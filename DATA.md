## About the synthetic data

Every identifier in the fixtures and the corpus is fabricated, and the generators make
them valid by construction: Luhn-valid card numbers with real network prefixes, mod-97-valid
IBANs, Verhoeff-valid Aadhaar-format numbers, NHS mod-11 numbers, ABA checksums, and SSNs
that follow SSA structural rules. Valid-looking numbers can coincide with real ones by
chance, but they are random draws, not real records. Names and cities come from short
hand-written lists of common first names, surnames and large cities. The dev and test
splits draw from disjoint halves of each list.

Total committed data: about 0.8 MB (vectors 364 KB, fixtures 460 KB, measured with `du -h`).
